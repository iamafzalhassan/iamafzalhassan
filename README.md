### Summary

I build mobile apps for real people, and I judge my work by how it feels in their hands. Over the past five years I have delivered apps for airlines, telecom providers, financial services and government, working closely with designers, testers and product teams to turn ideas into something people can trust. I care about the details users rarely notice but always feel, from how quickly a screen responds to how gracefully an app behaves when something goes wrong. I also enjoy guiding newer engineers and raising the standard of the work around me. The best part of the job is still the same. Someone picks up something I built, and it simply works.
